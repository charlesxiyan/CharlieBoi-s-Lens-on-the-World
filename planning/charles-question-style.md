# Charles question page style

Use this structure for pages credited to Charles (including jointly edited pages,
while preserving the other editor's contributions). Do not infer credits from Git.

1. One H1: Day number and question name.
2. Confirmed `*Edited by Charles*` credit.
3. Compact topic/method line and source link.
4. Problem in plain language, retaining all assumptions.
5. A tip callout for the core idea.
6. Short numbered reasoning under `## Short solution`, with display LaTeX equations.
7. A success callout containing the boxed answer.
8. A warning callout for a concrete common mistake.
9. Optional folded generalizations, checks, and original study records.
10. Relative navigation links.

Preserve existing study records. Never invent attempts, dates, difficulty labels,
or mastery values. Keep Sophia-only pages unchanged unless requested.

# About

Hi, I'm **Charles**, the person behind **CharlieBoi's Lens on the World**.

## Learning, one problem at a time

Here, I share LeetCode solutions and explanations, along with quantitative
finance interview questions and my approaches to solving them. I want to capture
not just the final answer, but the reasoning, insights, and lessons along the way.

After working through so many problems, I wanted a place to keep my thoughts
so I could return to them and review what I had learned. I also hope these notes
can help others working through similar challenges.

## Beyond the problem sets

Outside of studying, I enjoy gaming—especially **Genshin Impact** and
**Zenless Zone Zero**—traveling, taking road trips, and dancing **hip-hop**.
From time to time, I'll share experiences, observations, and discoveries from
my everyday life, too.

## Why this space exists

This website is both a record of my learning and a place to share it.
If an explanation here helps you understand a tricky problem or see an idea
from a different angle, then it has served its purpose.

Thanks for stopping by. I hope you find something useful—or simply something
you enjoy.

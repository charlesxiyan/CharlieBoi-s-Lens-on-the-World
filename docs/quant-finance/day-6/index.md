## Ideas to revisit

- For the expected minimum, sum tail probabilities: $E[\min]=\sum_k P(\min\ge k)$.
- Count poker hands by choosing the rank first, then the remaining card.
- Split a transport problem into stages, where each stage needs one fewer round trip.
- Given that Bob flipped more times, Alice's count is the minimum of two geometric variables.

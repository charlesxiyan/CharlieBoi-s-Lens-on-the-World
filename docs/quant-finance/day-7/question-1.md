# Day 7 · Drinking Dan

*Edited by Charles and Sophia*

**Brainteaser · Ratios · Conservation of volume**

[Original question on OpenQuant](https://openquant.co/questions/drinking-dan)

## Problem

Dan has two glasses. The first is $\tfrac34$ full of water and the second is empty.
He pours all the water from the first glass into the second, which then becomes
$\tfrac23$ full. What is the volume of the first glass divided by the volume of the second?

!!! tip "Core idea"
    Pouring doesn't change the amount of water. Write that amount in terms of each
    glass's volume and set the two expressions equal.

## Short solution

**1. Name the volumes.** Let $x$ be the volume of the first glass and $y$ the volume of the second.

**2. Write the same water two ways.** Before pouring, the water fills $\tfrac34$ of the first glass;
after pouring, it fills $\tfrac23$ of the second:

$$
\frac34x=\frac23y.
$$

**3. Solve for the ratio.**

$$
\frac{x}{y}=\frac{2/3}{3/4}=\frac23\cdot\frac43=\frac89.
$$

!!! success "Answer"
    $$\boxed{\frac{x}{y}=\frac89}$$

!!! warning "Watch out"
    Don't divide the fractions the wrong way round. The fuller fraction $\tfrac34$
    belongs to the **smaller** glass, so the first glass must be smaller and
    the ratio must be below 1. Equivalently, $y/x=\tfrac98$: the second glass
    is $12.5\%$ larger than the first.

??? note "Generalization: fraction a poured into a glass that becomes fraction b"
    If glass 1 is $a$ full and its water fills glass 2 to $b$ full, then

    $$
    a\,x=b\,y\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\frac{x}{y}=\frac{b}{a}.
    $$

    Here $a=\tfrac34$ and $b=\tfrac23$, so $x/y=\tfrac{2/3}{3/4}=\tfrac89$.

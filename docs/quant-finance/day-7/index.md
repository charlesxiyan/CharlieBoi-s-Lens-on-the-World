## Ideas to revisit

- The amount of water is fixed, so set the two fractions of volume equal: $\tfrac34V_1=\tfrac23V_2$.
- In a capped doubling game, each uncapped outcome contributes exactly $2^n\cdot2^{-n}=1$ to the expectation.
- Pattern $TH$ cannot overlap itself, so its waiting time splits into a wait for $T$ plus a wait for $H$.
- For the maximum of two dice, use $P(\max\le k)=(k/6)^2$ or sum tail probabilities.

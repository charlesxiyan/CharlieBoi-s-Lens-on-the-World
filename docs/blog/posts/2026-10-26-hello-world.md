# Hello, world: a place to think out loud

Every interesting project begins with a question. This site begins with one:
how can I turn the things I learn into something I can revisit and share?

<!-- more -->

## A notebook with room to grow

I want this to be a home for both short technical notes and longer reflections.
Some entries will explain a small piece of Python or C++. Others will work
through an equation, capture an experiment, or explore an idea beyond code.

The thread connecting them is curiosity. Writing makes me slow down, test my
assumptions, and explain what I think I understand.

## What comes next

- Keep useful examples small enough to run.
- Connect new ideas to older notes.
- Revisit explanations when experience changes my perspective.

This is the first page of a living notebook. Let's see where it leads.
